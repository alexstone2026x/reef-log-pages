Reef Aquarium Log

A simple journal for your reef aquarium.

In development Not yet available on the App Store.

Reef Aquarium Log is an iPhone app for owners of marine and reef aquariums who want to keep their water measurements in one place.

  • Log water tests for one or several aquariums
  • Follow changes on clear charts
  • Set your own target ranges
  • Keep local reminders for tests and maintenance
  • Save and restore a backup file
  • Works offline, with no account and no ads

Reef Aquarium Log is a record-keeping tool. It does not give advice about your animals or water chemistry.
