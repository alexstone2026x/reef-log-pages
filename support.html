Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. The app has no account and no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to another phone?

Open Settings, choose "Save a backup file" and keep the file somewhere safe (for example Files or iCloud Drive). On the other phone, open Settings and choose "Restore from a backup file".

I deleted an aquarium by mistake.

Open "Recently deleted" on the main screen and choose Restore. Deleted items stay there for 30 days.

Do the starting ranges give advice?

No. They are general reference values only, not advice. Every tank is different: set the ranges that suit your own tank, or turn a range off.
